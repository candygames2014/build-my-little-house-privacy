Privacy Policy

Application: Build My Little House
Developer: Wisimme
Effective date: October 16, 2026

Build My Little House is a free house-building and decoration game for children and adults. All nine special decorations are free. The game contains banner advertising, and does not offer rewarded videos, interstitial advertisements or in-app purchases in version 1.0.0.

Game progress and accounts

No account, name, email address or contact information is required to play. Houses, appearance choices, decorations and audio preferences are saved locally on your device. Wisimme does not operate a server receiving these saves. Android application backup is disabled. You can delete local progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Neutral age screen

At the start of a session, the app asks for a birth year. You can choose not to answer and continue playing. The entered year is validated in memory, then discarded. It is not saved or transmitted to Wisimme, Google or an advertising service. Every player receives the same game and child-directed advertising settings, regardless of their answer. The advertising SDK is initialized only after this screen is completed or skipped.

Banner advertising and information handled by Google

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display a banner at the bottom of the editor. All requests are tagged for child-directed treatment, non-personalized advertising and maximum ad content rating G. The Android advertising ID permission is removed. Child-directed tagging is applied to every request, including for older Android versions. Publisher first-party ID is disabled before loading advertisements. The app does not request precise or approximate location permissions, phone information, camera, microphone or contacts.

Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed. Google's SDK may automatically collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and application information, app-set or other device identifiers where applicable, app/ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google describes these practices as supporting advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The app does not enable collection of the Android advertising ID. Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Google's processing and retention are subject to Google's policies; Wisimme does not control Google's advertising records or their retention periods.

Children's privacy

The same child-directed protections apply to every user. We do not ask children for personal contact details or transmit their entered birth year or saved houses. The app uses Google's Families self-certified advertising SDK and no additional mediation adapters. Advertising remains subject to Google Play Families requirements and applicable children's privacy rules.

Privacy requests and deletion

For questions or privacy requests, contact wisimme@gmail.com. Local game data can be removed on your device. Requests concerning data held by Google must follow Google's privacy procedures; Wisimme cannot directly erase Google's advertising records. If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily include for handling your request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
