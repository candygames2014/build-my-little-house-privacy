Privacy Policy

Application: Build My Little House

Developer: Wisimme

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Wisimme respects the privacy of children and families. This policy explains how information is handled in Build My Little House, a free house-building and decoration game intended for children aged 12 and under.

1. Information and Game Progress

The game does not require account registration or ask players to provide their names, email addresses, or contact details. Houses, decorations, and unlocked rewards are saved locally on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The game uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements. Advertising is intended to be configured for child-directed treatment, with non-personalized ads and child-appropriate restrictions.

Google's advertising services may process technical information, such as IP addresses, device and application information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, for ad delivery, security, fraud prevention, and related purposes. The exact information processed depends on the advertising SDK and its configuration.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads privacy information.

3. Optional Rewarded Videos

Players may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to permanently unlock a special decoration. Watching these videos is voluntary, and the game remains playable without them.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is intended for children aged 12 and under. We do not require children to submit personal contact information. Advertising services must be configured in accordance with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies.

5. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not operate servers that receive players' saved houses or decorations. Google may process advertising-related information according to its applicable privacy and retention practices. Local game progress generally remains on the device until the application data is cleared or the game is uninstalled, subject to device backup settings.

6. Data Security

We seek to protect users' privacy by limiting the game's data collection and using established advertising services. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Privacy Requests

For questions about this policy or privacy-related requests, contact us at wisimme@gmail.com. Requests involving data processed by Google may also be subject to Google's privacy procedures.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the application, advertising services, or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
